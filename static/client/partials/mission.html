Welcome back! Our mission is to together create a new type of "Renaissance Man" using innovative peer-to-peer Web Technology.

About Us

We are aiming for a carefully curated Americana grassroots peer-to-peer digital library to establish widespread freedom of speech and data redundancy. Peer-to-peer technology allows for a Universal access and redundant "information fallback" that does not rely on a centralized server or any form of Gate-keeping authority other than operative (US) Law.

Free and Legal

propagate.info indexes infoHashes of public domain educational media, including and limited to Ebooks, Audiobooks, Classical Music, Documentaries, and Renaissance Art. This is not, never has been and never will be a piracy website. We rely on public domain educational media uploads curated by hand by our users.

WebTorrent-Enabled

Anyone anywhere in the world seeking Knowledge may freely download any torrent in the Browser without installing any Software by clicking [WebTorrent] under a Source.

Grassroots, Community-Driven

If you want to help build and establish access to Knowledge, either upload public domain media (if you can tag carefully and WebTorrent seed) or enter any legal swarm using a WebTorrent-enabled client.
